## Stage 13 — Payment / Shipping
Current business rules are explicit:
- Cash on delivery: enabled.
- Online payment: disabled until a real payment gateway is integrated.
- Shipping: free.
- The checkout UI no longer presents online payment as selectable.
- Server-side `create_order` remains authoritative and currently records shipping as 0.

This stage intentionally does not invent shipping fees or payment-gateway credentials.
